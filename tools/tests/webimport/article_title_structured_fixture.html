Example Journal

Reading widely is easy to plan and hard to sustain. A list of books grows faster than the hours available to read them, and a busy week can erase the habit entirely unless the workflow assumes that some days will be short.

The first change is to choose material before the week begins. Picking a single essay or a few chapters in advance removes the daily decision, and a decision removed is a chance to drift removed with it.

The second change is to read in small blocks that fit between other tasks. A block does not need to be long to count. What matters is that the page is opened and the thread of argument is carried forward, even if only for a few minutes.

Notes should be cheap to write. A sentence about what changed in the reader's understanding is more useful than a summary of what the page said, because the summary can be reconstructed later while the change cannot.

Finally, leave the next step visible. A bookmark, a short note, or a question at the end of a session turns the next block into a continuation instead of a restart, and restarts are where most reading habits lose their momentum.
