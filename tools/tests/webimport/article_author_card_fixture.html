How I Turn Learning Into a Workflow in the AI Era

Tw93 · 5 min read

Before AI, my way of learning was much more old school. I would read books, go through the blogs of well-known people in a field, and keep taking notes along the way. It was slow, but there was real joy in it.

Now that AI is here, my taste has not really changed. I still prefer to choose good material and spend real time with it. Collecting high-quality source material comes first, and then I read, filter, outline and write the article section by section.
