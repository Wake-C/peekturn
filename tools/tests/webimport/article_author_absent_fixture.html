Notes From a Reading Desk

A plain byline with no profile link · 4 min read

A page can describe an article without naming a person. It may credit no one, or it may present a byline as text that links nowhere. Neither is evidence of an author identity that the importer can honestly write into a book record.

The right outcome in that case is an empty creator, not a handle lifted from a URL and not the name of the site that happened to publish the page. A reader looking at the library should see the unknown-author state rather than a guess.

The article itself is still complete. Its headline and its body should survive import exactly as they did before this metadata was considered at all.

ARTICLE_AUTHOR_ABSENT_END: no author is fabricated for a page that never stated one.
