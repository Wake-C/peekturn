## Changes

- Remove whole-book audio preparation and the Live/Saved chooser; Listen now starts live narration directly.
- Keep live narration's upcoming-sentence preparation, buffering and cached replay, with the existing previous, play/pause and next controls.
- Remove the reader's top "Reading tools" title and close bar; centre taps still show or hide the bottom tools without moving the reading position.
- Remove repeated app names and page titles from Library, Statistics and Settings, keeping bottom navigation and the book-import button beside Shelf.

On upgrade, old whole-book audio is removed. Books, reading positions and the live sentence audio cache are retained.

### Download

Install `peekturn-1.3.1-11-release.apk` for Android 8.0 or later. The accompanying `.sha256` file can be used to verify the download.
