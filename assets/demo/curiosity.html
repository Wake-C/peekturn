A LITTLE EVERYDAY SCIENCE

Why leaves
look green.

Leaves absorb sunlight. A green pigment called chlorophyll helps plants use that light to make sugars from water and carbon dioxide.

Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light more strongly than green light. The green light reflected by a leaf is what reaches our eyes.
